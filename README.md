## How to use the webpage

- Visit [localhost:8080](http://localhost:8080/) (if run on port 8080)
- This will automatically redirect you to the login page
- Sign up with the link at the bottom of the page and then log in
- Click on the map at any location and fill in the necessary fields at the bottom to request a delivery
- Click 'dispatch' to send the drone
- View your orders with the account button at the top
